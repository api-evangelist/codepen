Rachel and Chris jump on the show to talk about a bit of client-side technology we use: Apollo . We use it because we have a GraphQL API and Apollo helps us write queries and mutations that go through that API. It slots in quite nicely with our React front-end, providing hooks we use to do the data work we need to do when we need to do it.
